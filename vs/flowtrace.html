AgendaBurn vs Flowtrace

Flowtrace is built for company meeting analytics and calendar policy. AgendaBurn is built to put a live dollar figure on one meeting. They overlap on “meeting cost,” but they are not the same product.

Facts below were checked on each vendor’s public website on November 19, 2026 (America/New_York). Pricing and features change; confirm details on the linked official pages before you buy. AgendaBurn is not affiliated with these companies.

Sources: flowtrace.co and flowtrace.co/pricing.

When Flowtrace is the better fit

  • You want meeting cost at the moment someone creates a calendar invite, inside Google Calendar or Outlook.
  • You need company meeting policy reminders, invite validation, or org-wide analytics (agenda rates, duration, recurring meetings, team benchmarks).
  • You already use Slack and want workplace surveys or employee meeting insights.
  • You need a vendor that publishes enterprise security documentation. Flowtrace says production runs in AWS Ireland, uses TLS in transit and AES-256 at rest, and that its controls map to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A and SOC 2 security, availability, and confidentiality principles. It does not, on the public pages we checked, describe a completed SOC 2 certification; ask Flowtrace for current audit status.

When AgendaBurn is the better fit

  • You want to show a climbing dollar figure to the people in the room, without installing an add-in or connecting a company calendar.
  • You are an individual or a small group (the free plan is capped at 3 attendees).
  • You want a lightweight Pro plan ($4.99/month) for calendar import, Slack sharing, AI debriefs, and PDF export, with free-tier salary data kept on the device.

AgendaBurn does not offer Flowtrace’s calendar audit, Slack analytics, meeting-policy enforcement, or enterprise procurement process. If that is the job, use Flowtrace.

FAQ

Is AgendaBurn a Flowtrace alternative?

Only for the narrow job of making meeting cost visible in the room. Flowtrace is a meeting analytics and transformation platform. AgendaBurn is a web calculator and timer.

How much does Flowtrace cost?

As of November 19, 2026, Flowtrace’s pricing page lists Free at £0 per user per month, Team at £1, Scale at £2, and Enterprise by quote, excluding VAT. Confirm current prices on flowtrace.co/pricing.

Does AgendaBurn integrate with Google Calendar?

Yes, on the Pro plan, along with Apple Calendar. That import loads a meeting into the timer; it is not a calendar add-in that shows cost on every invite the way Flowtrace does.
