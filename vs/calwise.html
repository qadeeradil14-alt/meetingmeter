AgendaBurn vs Calwise

Calwise is designed to show meeting cost in your calendar tools. AgendaBurn is designed to show meeting cost on a timer you start yourself. Both use salary-based math; the workflow is different.

Facts below were checked on each vendor’s public website on November 19, 2026 (America/New_York). Pricing and features change; confirm details on the linked official pages before you buy. AgendaBurn is not affiliated with these companies.

Sources: calwise.io and calwise.io/pricing.

When Calwise is the better fit

  • You want cost to appear automatically on calendar events, including before or as an invite is sent, without starting a separate timer.
  • Your team has more than 3 people who need this every day. Calwise’s free plan allows up to 10 users.
  • HR or finance needs department reports, benchmarks, or a digest email (Plus and Business plans).
  • You want salary data handled as an admin-controlled dataset on EU servers.

When AgendaBurn is the better fit

  • You do not want to upload a staff salary file or install a browser extension.
  • You want everyone in the room looking at one shared timer, including people who are not on the same calendar tenant.
  • You want AI meeting debriefs, Slack posting, or a PDF of that session (AgendaBurn Pro).

Calwise’s public site describes a calendar-and-extension analytics product, not a standalone in-room calculator. AgendaBurn does not currently offer Calwise’s department reports, industry benchmarks, or admin salary directory.

FAQ

Is Calwise or AgendaBurn better for small teams?

If you only need a timer for a meeting you are running, AgendaBurn is simpler and free for up to 3 attendees. If you want automatic cost on Google Calendar or Outlook for up to 10 people, Calwise’s free Basic plan is built for that.

How much does Calwise cost?

As of November 19, 2026, calwise.io/pricing lists Basic at €0 per month (up to 10 users), Plus at €30 per month (up to 30 users), and Business at €150 per month (up to 150 users), with custom Enterprise pricing. Confirm on that page.

Does AgendaBurn show cost inside Google Calendar?

No. Pro can import a calendar event into the AgendaBurn timer. It does not overlay cost on the calendar invite itself.
