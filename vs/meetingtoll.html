AgendaBurn vs MeetingToll

MeetingToll and AgendaBurn both try to change meeting behavior by showing a running dollar cost. One is a Chrome extension inside video calls. The other is a website you start yourself.

Facts below were checked on each vendor’s public website on November 19, 2026 (America/New_York). Pricing and features change; confirm details on the linked official pages before you buy. AgendaBurn is not affiliated with these companies.

Source: meetingtoll.com.

When MeetingToll is the better fit

  • Your meetings already happen in Zoom, Meet, or Teams and you want the cost meter inside that window.
  • You want a personal free plan that is not limited to 3 attendees (MeetingToll’s free plan is one user, not a 3-attendee cap).
  • You need a vendor that states SOC 2 Type II certification on its marketing site.
  • You want team analytics across video platforms after the meeting, with CSV export for leadership.

When AgendaBurn is the better fit

  • You cannot or do not want to install a Chrome extension (locked-down work browsers, or a TV / shared laptop in the room).
  • The meeting is in person, on the phone, or on a platform MeetingToll does not overlay.
  • You want per-person role and salary rates on one screen, plus optional AI debriefs and Slack/PDF sharing on Pro, at $4.99 per month.

These two are the closest products in this set because both emphasize a live, in-meeting cost figure. The practical difference is installation and where the number appears: a website versus a Chrome overlay in video calls.

FAQ

Is MeetingToll or AgendaBurn better for live meeting cost?

Both show a live cost. Pick MeetingToll if you want that number inside Zoom, Google Meet, or Teams via a Chrome extension. Pick AgendaBurn if you want a web page you can share on any screen, including in-person meetings.

How much does MeetingToll cost?

As of November 19, 2026, meetingtoll.com lists a free personal plan at $0 for one user and a Team plan at $12 per user per month, plus a higher plan at $20 per user per month. Confirm on their site, because the pricing module also includes other labels.

Does AgendaBurn work on Zoom?

You can run the AgendaBurn timer in a browser on a second screen or shared window during a Zoom call. It is not marketed as a Zoom app overlay the way MeetingToll is.
